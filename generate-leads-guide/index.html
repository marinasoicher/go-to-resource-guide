01 / Start here

Below are some tips to generate leads immediately so you can get momentum toward Core Rank (5 platform sign-ups or 1 Enagic sale) before your ads are up and running! Messy action is what this business is all about. While you'll of course be learning how to consistently generate leads for the long term, why not also get going now and get some results to build on?!

Remember, you don't have to be a pro to share about this business. The most important part is sharing your why and your excitement! People will connect with your passion and want to know what you're up to. You don't need to be an expert!

We recommend focusing on the actions below in your first 2-3 weeks or so, and then starting the other courses after you get going on these. You don't have to do all of them, but the more you do, the more initial momentum you'll build.

1

Finish the rest of the "Welcome" lesson you're currently in (5 more videos), and complete the next lesson ONLY: "Launching on Your Social Media" (3 videos). These 2 sections set you up to create momentum immediately!

03 / Do a launch post and follow up

2

Do a launch post on all of your social media! (AKA: take action on what you just watched above!) If you're open to it, you could even send an email or text to people who come to mind. Share the excitement and share why you started this. Don't talk about the business or the product, create curiosity! This isn't about plastering the products or business everywhere, it's just to get some initial traction. There are tips and launch post examples in the "Launching Your Business on Social Media" lesson.

2a

Follow up with anyone who shows interest from your launch post. If they say they're interested or engage with your post, engage back with them and send them a private message (examples below).

Example message

Be sure to adapt it to your own voice:

Copy and adaptHey, thanks for the comment! I'm doing this because [personal reason] (e.g., more time with kids / travel / extra $1K/mo). They are showing me exactly how to do it. I don't know if you've ever considered making extra money. If you're open, I'm happy to send you more information. If not, no worries

If they reply with some form of "no"

Ok cool. No problem. Do you know anyone that does want to make some extra money?

You'd think this is designed for referrals, but a lot of the time they'll come back with "Well, how does it work?" or "Does it cost anything?"

If they reply "yes" or "open": introduce the webclass

I have a free webclass that you can watch, it's what I watched and it helped me, and it'll do a good job to tell you if it's a fit for you or not. Let me know if you'd like me to send that.

When they say "send it": deliver it with a light call to action

Here it is: [link]. Let me know as soon as you've watched it.
Not at the BLC Ads Module yet?

If you don't have your own webclass link yet, send this link for them to watch the webclass (we recommend sending it personally, not posting it publicly): legacyonlinebusinessstrategy.com

Then use this sheet to check if they've registered or watched the webclass so you know when to follow up: Marina's Directs Webclass Views. Please ONLY follow up with leads on this sheet who you sent the link to. The other leads are from other members of the team.

FYI: they will never let you know! Always follow up, don't wait for them to get back to you.

Example follow-up message (next day)

Hey, let me know if you got a chance to watch the webclass.

If they say "No, I haven't watched it"

Hey, no problem. I have a lot of people who have watched the webclass that I just need to get back with, but let me know once you've watched it.

If they say "Yes, I watched it. And it was terrible."

Oh my goodness, did I send you the right link? Hold on a second. Ok, yes. I did. I'm surprised, what were you hoping to see?

If they say "Yes, I watched it."

Oh, cool. What did you like best about that?

Wait for a response. Then:

Why is that important to you?

Dig into the reason they gave for what they liked. Then:

Typically, when someone has shared the same things that you've just shared, they usually go ahead and jump into the discovery process. It's $99. And then we jump on a Discovery Call. And that's kind of the next step for someone that's saying what you're saying. And so, that's what I would suggest, that's what I did. And I can just tell you, I'm very grateful that I did. So that would be the next step for you, if you're wanting to move forward.

04 / Reach out to your network

3

Do you have anyone in your network who told you they wanted to earn more money, create a second stream of income, dislike their job, want to travel more, or want more freedom? If so, send them a message to tell them you've started an online business, that you're super excited because it'll allow you to create xyz (share your why), and that you thought about them because of xyz.

Example message. Adapt it to your own wording. You're talking to people you know, so make sure it sounds like you!

Hey NAME, how are you? I'm reaching out because I just started an online business because [personal reason] (e.g., more time with kids / travel / extra $1K/mo). They are showing me exactly how to do it. The other day you mentioned XYZ, and I don't know if you've ever considered making extra money. If you're open, I'm happy to send you more information. If not, no big deal.

When they say yes, use the messages in section 04 to send them the webclass and follow up.

05 / Reach out about health

4

Do you have anyone in your network who's a health advocate, has a specific health issue, or who you think the water could be a great benefit for? If so, send them a message to tell them you've started an online business or found an amazing health product, that you thought about them because of xyz, and that you think it might be able to help them. Ask if they'd be open to learning more about it.

06 / Ask your supporters

5

Do you have people in your circle who love you and want to support you? If so, tell them you've started an online business, you're not trying to recruit them, and you'd just love their support and a chance to practice a conversation with them. Ask if they'd be open to watching your webclass so they learn more about what you're doing. You'd love their positive feedback, and if they happen to know anyone looking for the solution we offer, they can refer them to you. You could even offer them a kick-back if they refer people, or take them to coffee! It's totally up to you and how you want to run your business. This is just a great way to get the ball rolling.
