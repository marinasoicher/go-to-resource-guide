We have so many incredible resources for you to find the answers to your questions. Please put effort into finding the answer yourself before asking questions in the group chats. Our priority is to coach and mentor our team, and our time isn't best used answering general questions in personal chats. Asking in the group chats means the rest of the team can learn the answers to those questions as well.

Be active in our community and in the chats!

The more energy you put into our community, the more you'll receive! Help and support each other.
