01 / How to use this template

This verbiage is a starting point, not a script to send without thinking.

Read each message before you send it and make sure you understand what you're saying and why. Every conversation is different. Your lead might be a nurse, a stay-at-home mom, or a dad working two jobs, and the message should fit the person you're talking to.

Make it yours

Anything in [brackets] is a spot to fill in yourself before you send: your name, your links, your lead's name, or a piece of Marina's story and numbers that you replace with your own. The brackets come with it when you tap Copy, so you can't miss them.

Your story is what makes these messages work. If Marina's message says she left her corporate job and you left nursing, say nursing. If you haven't hit a result yet, talk about what you're building and why, never a number that isn't yours.

02 / Sales tips to keep in mind

Sales is communication. The messages below only work when you know who you're talking to and where they are. Before you reply to a lead, ask yourself:

1

Who am I talking to?

What do they do for work, what does their family look like, and what made them click? Look back at their application and your earlier messages.

2

What do they need?

More time with their kids, out of a job that's burning them out, extra income so money stops being tight. Listen for it in their own words.

3

What do I offer, and how does it fill that gap?

Connect what they told you to what this business gives them. Use their words back to them, not a pitch.

4

Where are they in the process?

Someone who hasn't watched the webclass needs a different message than someone who watched it and is deciding. The sections below follow the stages in order, so find their stage first.

08 / Common questions and objections

"Who is this?"

This is [your full name]. You clicked on my ad and filled out an application to learn more about possibly starting your own online business to own your time and finances. Is that still something you'd like to explore?

"How fast will I see results?"

That's a great question. This business takes energy, time, and a willingness to be coachable. Every person's timeline is different. Some people come in and start seeing results in the first 1-2 months, while others take more time depending on how much energy and focus they can put into it. If you're consistent and willing to learn, we recommend giving yourself about 6-12 months to build a solid foundation and start creating sustainable results. This isn't a get-rich-quick thing - it's about setting yourself up for long-term freedom and even generational wealth for your family. What kind of timeline are you personally hoping for when it comes to reaching your goals?

They watched the webclass and say they don't have money to invest

This one is a back-and-forth. Wait for their answer before each next line.

You: Totally get that. Let me ask you something, if money wasn't a factor right now, do you feel like this business would actually help you? (Insert why: freedom, income, time with family, etc.) Them: Yes. You: Can I make a suggestion? Them: Yes. You: 99% of us, including myself, didn't have the money to invest when we started. That's actually the exact reason we started... because we were tired of being in a situation where we couldn't afford to do anything to better our lives. This business is literally designed for people who don't have money and want to change that. So let me ask you this: if we could help you get started even with little to no money down, and this could radically change your life and give you real freedom... would that be worth exploring? Them: Yes. You: Okay perfect. Then the first step is finding a way to sign up for the $99 which would be the only upfront investment. Then me or one of my specialists will help you to get started with little to no money down once you're in the discovery process. Is your vision for your future here strong enough to find a way to get that $99?

They ask about the investment before watching the webclass

At this point, you don't even know yet if this is something that's for you so right now, there's actually no cost to you. This short video explains exactly how you can build a full time income from home and break free from [nursing], and then if it resonates, we can talk about investment options after. Can you commit to prioritizing 30 minutes to watch it and see if this is even something that could help you?

Or, if money being tight is the real worry:

So if you're tight on money, that's clearly one reason why you clicked my ad in the first place. If you want real change and to not be tight on money forever, you have to be willing to learn & explore ways to do things differently, despite not having much money right now. So instead of letting your situation hold you back from learning, it should be fueling you to learn new ways. Otherwise you'll never actually get out of that "tight on money" cycle. I only say that because I know the cycle and have been there. I decided to break that cycle with this business but it started with my mindset. Does that make sense?

"How much do we invest after the $99?"

Yeah so after you go through the discovery process and have your 1:1 call with us, and decide this is something you want to move forward with you would purchase your products. This is affiliate marketing which means in order to sell the high ticket product, you just have to own it yourself (we don't have to stock inventory or pay monthly fees). There are a variety of starting options at this point depending on your situation & goals which you will go over on your 1:1 call. None of us had the money just laying around to invest into these products and we don't expect you to either. We help you find financing so you invest as little as zero out of pocket right away. But, you won't even know if this is for you or not until you go through the discovery process and learn all the details. If you decide it's not for you you will get your $99 refunded as long as you complete the videos and coaching call within two weeks of signing up. I recommend starting there since it's totally risk-free and you can get all the info and make an informed decision on if this might be what you're looking for. Does that feel like a good next step?

"What will financing look like?"

I hear you. I want to be very transparent here. This process cannot be explained in a text. I'd be disrespecting this business if I tried to turn our 3 hour training that you'll go through into a simple text. The purpose of the discovery process is to allow you to fully understand this business and the financing before you make any real commitments. Like I said, paying the $99 is just a step to learn more but legally I have to give you a refund for two weeks after you sign up if you decide this isn't for you. I only want to work with people who align here and see the long term vision. You will go through the training videos where you'll learn more about the product, the compensation plan & how quickly you can see your return on investment, and how to start IF you decide to move forward. Then, you'll get a 1:1 call with me or my assistant to go over any questions you have and we will give you our best advice for financing. I wish I could just tell you what your financing would look like but it's different for everyone since I don't know your financial situation. Does that make sense?

Not interested

If you're open to sharing, what about this business isn't for you? Your feedback will help me bring more aligned people in who do feel this is for them.

Interested later in life

In the meantime, my best advice is to stay up on my weekly emails from - [your email address] - to stay inspired and connected while you figure things out. Reach out when you're ready.
