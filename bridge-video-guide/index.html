01 / Before you hit record

Your bridge video gets its own page in your funnel. After people opt in on your landing page, they'll watch your bridge video, and then it links them to the longer masterclass. This is your time to shiiine!

Before you hit record, I highly recommend taking a few moments to drop in, ground yourself, and connect to your ICA (ideal client avatar). Think about who they are. Where they're stuck. What they're feeling right now. And who you were before everything changed. When you create this video from that heart-centered place, your energy will translate, and your viewer will feel you instantly. So yes, of course you can take this framework to your ChattyG, but I highly recommend you take the time to sit with your ICA without your robot ;)

02 / Why a bridge video?

A bridge video is a short intro video that "bridges the gap" between the ad your prospect clicked on (or the conversation you just had with them) and the full masterclass you're directing them to.

Think of it as the digital handshake. It reassures your viewer that they're in the right place, warms them up to the host, and gives them a reason to keep watching.

Why it works

  • Builds trust through relatability
  • Frames the training video as valuable and relevant
  • Handles initial skepticism
  • Positions the viewer to say "this is for me"

1Speak to the former you

Market to the version of you before you took action. That's your ICA.

"I was burned out, anxious, and wondering if I'd ever get ahead..."

2Don't assume, empathize

Use non-definitive language. Don't tell them who they are, invite them to relate.

"If you're anything like me... you might be feeling stuck, tired, or skeptical."

3Relatability over résumé

No one cares about your accolades. They care about connection.

"I was where you might be. I didn't have it all figured out. But I took a step."

4Set the stage, then step aside

You're not the star, the presentation is. Get them excited to watch (and be sure to introduce the host, so they know what to expect).

"The info you're about to hear totally changed how I looked at earning online..."

04 / Tips for filming

  • Keep it under 2 minutes
  • Film landscape (horizontal) and center yourself
  • Find natural light (like a window or shaded outdoors)
  • Speak clearly, like you're talking to one person
  • Be authentic. If you're nervous, own it! It makes you real.
  • Practice makes powerful. Even with the teleprompter hacks below, your connection, cadence, and confidence are what will land. Make it sound like you're chatting with a friend, not reading.
  • Breathe into your belly, speak from your truth, and let your gestures flow naturally. This isn't about being perfect, it's about being real.

06 / Bonus: ninja marketing moves

Advanced psychology and marketing upgrades for your script.

1Name the inner dialogue

Speak directly to the conversation already happening in their mind. This creates a mirror effect and deep resonance.

"You might be wondering... 'Is this actually legit?' or 'Can I really do this?' I asked the same thing."

Why it works: it builds immediate trust and signals "they get me."

2Create tension, then offer relief

Use contrast between where they are now and what's possible.

"I used to wake up dreading Mondays, barely scraping by. Now, I choose where I work, when I work, and who I work with."

Why it works: it triggers desire and urgency in a subtle, story-driven way.

3Normalize skepticism

Instead of brushing it off, acknowledge it and lean in.

"At first I thought it might be a scam. But something told me to listen with an open mind, and I'm so glad I did."

Why it works: it reduces resistance. They feel safe to stay curious.

4Give a specific reason to watch now

Don't just say "watch the video." Frame it as timely and relevant.

"If you've been circling the same goals with no real traction, this might be the shift you didn't know you needed."

Why it works: it creates urgency without hype.

5Include a micro call to action

After setting up trust and curiosity, guide them clearly.

"You'll see why this works, and how it could work for you too. So hit play, and I'll meet you there."

Why it works: it directs attention and removes friction.

6Frame the video as a gift

People hate being sold to, but they love discovering new possibilities.

"This gave me back my freedom, and it might do the same for you."

Why it works: it shifts the energy from pitch to invitation.

07 / ChatGPT prompt for your script

Before you start

When using ChatGPT, don't just take the first thing it gives you and call it done. Read through it, feel into it, and decide if it fully aligns and covers everything in the layouts here. The robots are helpful, but not perfect, and not you. Ask it to change things or give it more direction until you have something you love that feels natural for you.

1

Copy everything on this page (all the tips, outlines, and examples above) and paste it into ChatGPT.

2

Then copy and paste the message below and fill in the blanks.

Copy, paste, and fill inHey ChatGPT! I've pasted in a guide that explains how to create a powerful bridge video for my funnel. I need your help writing my personalized bridge video script based on the guide I just shared. Here's the information you'll need about me: My Ideal Client Avatar (ICA): [Describe your ICA here: who they are, what they're struggling with, and what they deeply desire. Example: People who feel stuck in the 9-5 system, know they're meant for more, and are ready to explore their purpose and create a life of freedom and alignment.] My Personal Story: [Share a few details about you: where you were before you found this business, what your life looked like, how you felt, and why you decided to take action. Be specific so it feels real and relatable.] My Mentor's Story: [Your mentor's name and story. Example: Marina, a mama of two, was working 60+ hours a week while feeling like life was passing her by. Her deepest desire was to be present with her kids while still creating a life of freedom and purpose for her family. She discovered a simple way to build an online business that gave her that freedom, and now she helps others do the same.] Here's what I'd like you to do: - Write a 60-90 second bridge video script for me using the guide and examples I provided. - Make it sound warm, natural, and conversational, like I'm speaking directly to one friend. - Use empathy and understanding rather than hype or sales language. - Flow from my story to my mentor's story, and then set up the free training as the natural next step. - End with a clear, simple call to action inviting them to watch the training.
