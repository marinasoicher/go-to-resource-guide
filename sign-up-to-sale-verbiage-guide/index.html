03 / Reminders to book the call

Book call reminder 1

I'm sure you're enjoying the Discovery Process and are seeing how having a high ticket business & passive income can totally change your life. Quick reminder to book your call asap as our calendar fills quickly. Here's your booking link: [your booking link]

Book call reminder 2

Hi NAME, I noticed you haven't scheduled your call yet. Sometimes when we take action towards real change in our lives, like investing to learn how to earn online, it's so easy to let it slip by just because it's new and maybe scary. Make sure you get logged in, book your call, and stay in that momentum!

Book call reminder 3

Just one more reminder here to get your 1:1 call booked. Remember, to have this call you will need to have completed all of the Discovery Process videos if you haven't yet. I'm dropping the call booking link here to make it easier for you, chat soon NAME: [your booking link]

Haven't booked the call: release attachment

Sorry, I haven't seen you book your call yet so this is my last message until you take action for yourself. If you're ready to take the next step and start making $3,000 per sale online, message me here or book your 1:1 call. I'd love to help you hit the ground running. There's so much opportunity waiting for you! Looking forward to hearing from you when you're ready.

04 / Once the call is booked

Call booking confirmation

Yay NAME, your 1:1 call is booked. I have a team of advisors helping me take these calls. Your advisor will hop in here and say hi soon. Looking forward to supporting you on this journey!

Second message

Some important things to note for your call: 1. If you no show to your call, you waive your right to a refund and we won't book another call. 2. Your Clarity Questionnaire must be submitted within 24 hours before your call time (find this in module 1). 3. All Discovery Process videos (2 modules) must be fully watched by your call. 4. Your time is valuable and so is ours. You can reschedule or cancel your call up to 24 hours in advance.

05 / Call reminders

48 hours before

Dropping a little reminder here for our call in 2 days! WHEN: [call day and time, in their time zone] (45-60 min) Be sure to complete your questionnaire and all the videos before your call time. If you're unable to make your scheduled call or need more time to complete the videos/questionnaire, notify me ASAP to reschedule as we do not reschedule with less than 24 hours notice.

24 hours before

Your strategy call is in 24 hours. Have you completed the questionnaire and all the videos in the discovery process? Please confirm yes or no.

1 hour before

Can't wait to meet with you in an hour! Make sure all Discovery Process videos are complete and your questionnaire is filled out. If you have not completed these, please let me know ASAP. The number we'll be calling from is in your email. See you soon!

Questionnaire reminder

Send this if they haven't filled out the questionnaire before the call:

Morning NAME, I'm not seeing your answers from the questionnaire yet and that's a requirement for us to have our call today. Please fill this out asap and send me a message once you do. If I don't see that before our call time, I'll have to cancel our call & reschedule so we can be sure that's done. Thanks!

06 / If they cancel and don't rebook

Follow-up 1

Hey NAME, I saw you just cancelled your call but I don't think you rescheduled that yet. I know life happens. Were you planning on rescheduling your call with us or did something come up that made you change your mind?

Follow-up 2

Just reconnecting again to make sure everything is okay on your end? I haven't heard from you since you cancelled your call.

Follow-up 3: release attachment

NAME, when you're ready to see what's possible with this online business and powerful community to build with, here's the link to rebook your call: [your booking link]

07 / If they don't show

Personalize this one based on what you know about them, if you can.

NAME, you booked this call with us today because [refer to their questionnaire answers or your previous conversation, if possible]. Since you didn't show up to our call today, I just wanna make sure you're okay on a human level and see if anything has changed for you since booking your call?
