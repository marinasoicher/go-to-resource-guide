01 / You just got a sign-up

Send your sign-up these two messages. The first one acknowledges their sign-up. Send the second one right after (don't wait for a response).

Message 1Hi NAME! Congratulations on taking the first step, you're officially in the discovery process and I'm so excited for you. The videos inside are going to walk you through how people just like you & me are earning $3K-$8K per sale with a simple online business and fully automated systems that work around the clock, even while they sleep. First things first: check the email we just sent you and create your account. (Be sure to peek in your junk folder just in case!)
Message 2 (send right after)Here's exactly what to do next: Step 1: Watch the training videos & fill out the clarity questionnaire Set aside 3 hours to go through all the information, this is where everything clicks. You'll walk away knowing exactly how this simple online business works and what your income potential looks like. Step 2: Book your 1:1 strategy call This call is YOUR time to ask questions, get clarity, and walk away with a personalized startup plan built around your life and goals. All the videos and questionnaire must be done before your call so be sure to choose your call day/time with that in mind: Use this link to book with me [insert your own call calendar link]

02 / Good to know while they're in discovery

  • While your lead is in the Discovery Process, check in often if you don't hear from them or see them book their call. Use the Sign Up To Sale General Verbiage & Important Links doc for follow-up verbiage and extra important links.
  • Message or email them the Zoom link for their call as soon as you see they've booked it. If you're a software owner, you can automate this. Otherwise, you may want to use a different platform to automate it.

03 / Sales training refresher

You should already be through this, but it doesn't hurt to go through it again.

04 / After the call

  • Get them into Telegram if you want, or keep them wherever you were already communicating (text, Instagram, Facebook). This part is up to you.
  • Send them any resources you discussed on the call: ROI graphics, the Power of the Quad graphic, the commission structure graphic, the Launch Guide, direct links to the financing options you discussed, their investment price graphics, or any water resources (like the water documentary). Download the ROI and package graphics.
