Tips for applying for credit cards

1

Apply to two right in a row

Most people need two cards for their full investment, since credit card companies don't tend to give high limits right off the bat. If you wait too long, you might get denied, so apply to one right after the other or at the same time. If you do get denied by one, stop applying immediately.

2

Our recommendations

For good or excellent credit: Capital One Venture, Chase Freedom, Discover, and then Citi. You also get cash back for spending the whole limit on these, so it's a win-win! And they're all 0% for 12 to 15 months (see the links below).

3

Once you're approved, ask for a limit increase

You can call the company and ask for a limit increase if you need one, and they might give it to you. This short video explains how to do that. You can also ask them to expedite your card (they shouldn't charge for this) so you don't have to wait as long for it in the mail. We know you're itching to get going, we felt the same way!

4

Talk with your coach

Your coach can give you more credit card application tips based on your situation.

3. Credit cards for building credit No credit or 300 to 689

1

Apply for the card that best suits your situation from Best Credit Cards for No Credit.

2

Once you're approved, put as many daily purchases on it as you can: food, groceries, gas, your water bill, your phone bill.

3

Every two weeks, pay the entire balance off. This builds your credit quickly and gives the companies evidence of on-time payments.

4

Every month or so, check your credit score through Credit Karma.

Your goal

Once we get you to 650 or above, you'll be able to apply for a bigger loan for your quad or trifecta and be more likely to get approved!

02 / Canada

Canadians only

Please send your coach a photo of your driver's license. This helps the government verify it's you when you start this business, and it MUST be submitted with your forms.

1. 0 to 1% interest credit card

MBNA is the only credit card in Canada with a 0% interest rate, and BMO is a very low (0.99%) interest option. They're both balance transfer cards: you pay for your products with another credit card, then transfer that balance to the MBNA or BMO card for a 0 to 1% interest rate for 9 to 12 months. They can be a great option to use together or to combine with another loan, line of credit, or Medicard.

  • 0% MBNA Mastercard: apply now
  • 0.99% BMO Mastercard: apply now

2. Loans

A loan is one of the most common ways any business gets up and running, because it's the quickest way to receive funds. You will pay interest, but the idea is to pay the loan off much faster as you build your business and start bringing in revenue. Here are our favorite options:

  • GoPeer: low interest rates for good credit (you need 3+ years of credit history). Apply now
  • Tangerine: apply for a line of credit
  • Borrowell: quick loans up to $20,000. Apply now
  • Fairstone: Canada's leading non-bank lender, with loans from $500 to $35,000. Apply now

3. Your bank

If you have a good relationship with your bank, talk with them about what they can do. They could increase your current credit limit, open a new line of credit for you, or give you a loan!

4. Medicard

Medicard does loans for medical equipment and works with Enagic regularly. We've had many people use them to get a quad or trifecta! Be aware that their interest rates can be quite high. You can negotiate and ask them to lower it. Most of their loans are for 5 or 6 years, so ideally you'd pay it off faster than that to pay less interest.

Good to know

Medicard only asks for your employment information to confirm your income. They WILL NOT tell your boss or manager that you're starting a business (they aren't legally allowed to).

After Medicard approves you and gives you your interest rate, contact them by phone or email and ask for a lower rate. In many cases they'll lower it significantly, all you need to do is ask!

04 / Europe (UK & Ireland)

Please take note

In the UK, it's best to split your purchase between 2 cards. If you apply for 2 x £5k at the same time, they won't realize you've got the other application in. Have 2 tabs open and click the final submit button on one right after the other.

There is currently no VAT on the product. It's added after purchase and paid when the product is delivered to your door. It varies a little but is around 20%: someone will arrive at your door and you'll pay the tax.

Check your credit: experian.co.uk

1. Money Saving Expert 0% for 12 to 24 months

Go to the search box on moneysavingexpert.com and type "0% money transfer." Most people are eligible if you have a job and have been living in the UK for a few years.

There are cards with no interest for 12, 18, 24 and 36 months, with a very small fee. Usually the best choices are:

  • MBNA
  • Tesco Bank
  • Virgin Money

2. Money Supermarket

Moneysupermarket.com offers loans, personal loans, and bad or refused credit loans, plus regular credit cards, credit building cards, 0% interest cards, cashback cards, bad credit cards and more.

3. Ireland

In Ireland, the best way to compare credit cards and loans is switcher.ie or bonkers.ie. You can also apply through your own bank or the Credit Union.
