02 / If you're using forms

Go to enagic.com > EWS Login > Enagic Forms, select the country where the buyer lives, and download the forms.

2

Look in the country's folder (for example "Canada") and check that country's checklist to see exactly which forms you need. The Sales by Country Guide lists them too.

3

You can save the forms on your computer, but re-download them once or twice a month, because Enagic keeps updating them.

03 / Where each product goes

Selling a Trifecta or a Quad

You're selling an Anespa to your new business partner, so the new person is the applicant and you are the sponsor.

  • If you started with a K8 (or K8 and Ukon), the Anespa goes in the 1A lane of your K8.
  • If you started with a Trifecta, it goes in the 2A lane of your Anespa.
  • If you started with a Quad (and stacked deep), it goes in the 1A lane of your Ukon.

For a Trifecta, your business partner is selling themselves the K8 and the Ukon, so they're the applicant and also the sponsor. The K8 goes in their first 1A lane and the Ukon in their second 1A lane of their Anespa.

For a Quad, the 2 K8s go in the two 1A lanes of their Anespa, and the Ukon goes in the 2A lane of their Anespa.

Which ID to sell under

If you started with a Quad where your Ukon was in the 1A lane, stack all sales under your Anespa ID until 4A.
A TrifectaYour Anespa is your top account. Use this ID for all sales until you hit 4A and open a new Ukon account (the 4A hack).

05 / What to send your new partner

USA

Send this once the first product is ordered, before you log in to their DSP account to place their other products (for Duo, Trifecta, and Quad packages):

Copy and adaptNAME, your anespa is ordered! I have a few things I need from you before I can complete the rest of your package. - You'll be getting an email from Enagic with an ID # and password. When you get that email, login to the Enagic portal and add your bank account that you want your commissions to go to. You will get a pop up asking you to do this right when you login & it takes just a few minutes. - Once you've done that, send me the ID number here and confirm that you've added your bank info. Then I can go into your account and complete your package! Here's the link to the Enagic portal: https://information.enagic.com/en/login

Canada

Send this with their forms. This example is for a Trifecta, so adjust it to the order.

Copy and adaptHere are your Trifecta forms! The Anespa is filled in with my info under the sponsor info section since that's your first purchase and I am selling that to you, and then you will be your own sponsor for the K8 and Ukon Sigma since you're technically buying those from yourself (so you'll put your name & info in the sponsor spot and also sign as the sponsor for those). Since we are doing Medicard, you only need to put in card information for your Ukon and not the Anespa or K8, and that card will not be charged - they just require to have it on file for renewal purposes. WHEN YOU ARE FINISHED FILLING OUT THE FORMS PRESS THE ORANGE "DONE" BUTTON AT THE TOP I will also need you to send me a picture of your ID if you haven't already Please note the Ukon is 6 months delayed on shipping So excited for you, let me know on Telegram if you have any questions!

Delete the Medicard paragraph if they aren't using Medicard.
