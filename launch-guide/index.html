04 / Unsupportive loved ones?

Sometimes in this business we're so excited, but we don't have support from our significant other or parents or loved ones. Usually what happens is we get excited and want to share, but they haven't seen the information. They want to protect you, so they start asking questions and seem skeptical.

Just know that you are not alone. This happens to many people when they start their business. Our best advice: share your passion and excitement with your loved one, but don't try to convince them. When they see how happy and excited you are, they might be willing to say "I trust you" and sit back while you go after it.

You also always have the option of sharing the Discovery Process with them if you feel they'd like to see it all laid out the way you did. This can be really helpful for getting husbands on board, because they can see it all for themselves.
